# Right Thurr Free Game Prelaunch

## Position

Right Thurr is a Thurr-led Free Game brand for ambitious operators.

It should help people get put on to practical tools, habits, places, people, systems, and setups that make life and work move better. The brand can include travel, fitness, healthy food, AI tools, workflows, creator/operator setups, and people doing useful things.

This is not a store-first launch. The first 30 days are an audience and trust test. Commerce, paid community, and heavier product work wait until the audience proves what it wants.

## Audience

Start with ambitious operators:

- People trying to improve life, money, health, workflow, and network.
- People curious about AI but not trying to become full-time technical builders.
- Creators, founders, students, young professionals, and local operators who want useful recommendations they can act on.
- People who already know Thurr as someone who travels, connects people, works out, builds with AI, and points people in the right direction.

Do not narrow the first lane to AI builders only. AI is a major pillar, but the bigger promise is practical Free Game.

## Brand Stack

Use this hierarchy:

- Thurr: the face and trust layer.
- Right Thurr: the Free Game, lifestyle, content, community, product, and merch layer.
- Thurr Solutions: the B2B AI agency and implementation layer.

One piece of content can connect all three, but each surface needs a clear lead. A travel post with a useful AI setup is Right Thurr-led. A client automation case study is Thurr Solutions-led. A personal lesson from the road is Thurr-led.

## Content Pillars

Use this mix for the first 30 days:

- 40% Free Game: AI tools, workflows, setups, resources, apps, templates, and practical put-ons.
- 25% Lifestyle Proof: travel, workouts, healthy eats, family, network, and day-in-the-life context.
- 20% Build In Public: Thurr Solutions lessons, automation builds, client-safe process breakdowns, and behind-the-scenes systems.
- 15% Community Seed: questions, polls, challenges, member spotlights, wins, and "what do you need help with?" prompts.

## Free Game Formats

Rotate these formats so the feed feels useful instead of random:

- Tool Put-On: one AI tool, what it does, who it helps, how Thurr uses it, and one practical setup.
- Setup Tour: desk, Mac Mini, phone, gym bag, travel bag, creator kit, AI workflow, or meal-prep setup.
- Healthy Eats: airport food, quick meals, grocery finds, meal-prep moves, and better choices while traveling.
- Workout Clip: routine, form note, recovery tip, hotel gym session, or consistency lesson.
- Travel Lesson: what a place taught, how to move smarter, what to pack, who to meet, and what to avoid.
- People Doing Things: spotlight someone building, creating, training, serving, selling, teaching, or connecting people.
- Build Lesson: a client-safe AI/workflow lesson from Thurr Solutions without private data or inflated claims.
- Community Prompt: ask what people are trying to get put on to right now.

## AI Tool Board Rule

AI tools are a rotating board, not a permanent stack.

Before publishing a specific AI tool recommendation, check whether the tool is still active, priced reasonably, and relevant. Each tool post should answer:

- What it does.
- Who it helps.
- How Thurr would use it.
- One practical setup someone can copy.
- Any caution, cost, or limitation.

Do not imply that a tool guarantees money, growth, automation success, or business results.

## Community Shape

Start with a light Free Game Circle, not a heavy paid community.

Minimum viable community:

- Weekly put-on thread.
- Weekly AI tool or setup breakdown.
- Monthly challenge around fitness, content, workflow, or business systems.
- Member wins and useful people spotlights.
- One simple intake question: "What are you trying to get put on to right now?"

Do not promise daily support, private coaching, constant audits, or hands-on builds inside the free circle.

## Community Timing

Wait to launch a paid or high-touch community until these signals exist:

- 8-12 weeks of consistent content.
- People ask to join, learn, share setups, or get the links.
- 100+ warm email, SMS, or community leads.
- Repeated questions appear often enough to organize into a shared room.
- There is a simple support/moderation process that does not create daily burden for Thurr.

Until then, collect interest and keep the community lightweight.

## First 30-Day Test

Goal:

Validate which Right Thurr pillar earns real attention before launching commerce or paid community.

Track:

- Saves.
- Replies.
- DMs.
- Comments.
- Shares.
- Email signups.
- "Send me that" requests.
- People asking for links, setups, tools, meal ideas, workouts, or community access.

Review after 30 days:

- Which pillar got the strongest response?
- Which format was easiest for Thurr to sustain?
- Which questions repeated?
- Which posts created business leads for Thurr Solutions?
- Which products or merch ideas came up naturally?

## First 30-Day Success Criteria

The prelaunch works if it creates:

- 30 posted or drafted content pieces.
- 10+ direct responses or useful conversations.
- 25+ warm waitlist or interest-list entries.
- 3 repeat content themes worth turning into a weekly series.
- 1 clear next test: waitlist landing page, free circle, first merch sample, or AI setup guide.

## Boundaries

Do not:

- Launch Shopify, Printful, Printify, Stripe, Twilio, or paid ads yet.
- Launch a paid community yet.
- Claim guaranteed income, fitness, health, travel, or business outcomes.
- Publish client-sensitive details, private screenshots, credentials, webhook URLs, or internal notes.
- Use fake testimonials, fake scarcity, synthetic endorsements, or compliance-risky product claims.
- Blur Right Thurr with Thurr Solutions on B2B client pages.
