## Locked Specifications

Material:

- Full-grain real leather.
- Not PU.
- Not bonded leather.
- Not "genuine leather" as the quality claim.
- Supplier must state leather grade and origin in reply.

Colorways:

- Rich clay orange.
- Deep black.
- Orange must feel premium, warm, and grown-up.
- Do not use bright/toy/safety orange for leather.
- Pantone target range to discuss: PMS 1675 C / 7586 C.
- Bright orange can be used for website, stickers, and packaging accents only.

Edge finish:

- Painted edge.
- Clean painted edge is preferred over stitched-only finishing for the premium sample.

Hardware:

- Sample both gunmetal and gold/brass if possible.
- Founder leans gunmetal, but compare both in hand before locking.
- Buckle strap closure.

Deboss - face:

- `thurr` wordmark.
- Lower third of front face.
- Clean single mark only.
- Do not deboss `HURR OR THURR` on the supplier sample face.

Deboss styles:

- Sample both blind/tonal deboss and gold-foil deboss if possible.
- Final choice happens after physical comparison.

Strap mark:

- Small RT monogram or `T` mark on the buckle strap.
- Subtle, as in render.
- Optional if it adds cost; quote separately.

Series mark:

- `Series 001` debossed small at base.
- Tonal and restrained.

Privacy flap:

- Yes.
- Snap-closed flap over a clear ID window/card slot.
- Must feel premium, not like a cheap plastic luggage insert.

NFC chip:

- NTAG215 preferred.
- Embedded, not visible.
- Encoded to a static URL provided by Thurr, such as `https://thurr.co/t/001`.
- Must read reliably through the leather and flap.

Dimensions:

- Approximately 4.3 in x 2.7 in body.
- Approximately 110 x 68 mm body.
- Standard premium luggage-tag size.
- Supplier should confirm nearest stock tooling size.

Optional custom shape:

- Ask for a second quote for an arrow-shaped version as a comparison only.
- The standard rounded-rectangle tag remains the primary Series 001 sample.
- Arrow shape must not weaken the object, reduce NFC reliability, or force the privacy flap/ID window to feel cheap.
- Supplier must state custom cut-die/tooling cost, custom-shape MOQ, sample lead time, edge-paint durability around the point, NFC chip placement, and dieline requirements.
- Dieline assumption: 3 mm bleed, 5 mm safe margin, 8 mm preferred tip radius, 6 mm absolute minimum tip radius, and no metal hardware within 15 mm of the chip.

## Supplier Questions

Ask every supplier:

1. Can you confirm full-grain leather? State grade and origin.
2. What is MOQ and per-unit price at 100 / 150 / 250 units, with and without NFC?
3. What is the per-unit upcharge and added lead time for made-to-order personalization/initials?
4. Can you use NTAG215 and confirm read reliability through leather plus flap?
5. What is sample cost, sample shipping cost, and sample turnaround time?
6. What is production lead time after sample approval?
7. Can packaging be quoted separately: rigid box, dust bag, insert cards?
8. Can you Pantone-match the clay leather to a swatch?
9. Can you sample both gunmetal and gold/brass hardware?
10. Can you sample both blind/tonal deboss and gold-foil deboss?
11. Can you add `Series 001` debossed small at the base?
12. Can you add a subtle RT/T mark on the strap, quoted separately?
13. What is your defect/replacement policy?
14. Optional comparison: can you quote a custom arrow-shaped version, including tooling cost, custom-shape MOQ, edge durability, privacy flap feasibility, NFC placement, and dieline requirements?

- Series 001 debossed small at the base

For the sample, please make it in clay orange and include both a gunmetal and a gold/brass hardware option if possible. Please also include both a blind/tonal deboss and gold-foil deboss example if possible so I can compare in hand.

Please reply with:

1. confirmation of full-grain leather, including grade and origin
2. sample cost, sample shipping, and sample turnaround time
3. per-unit price at 100 / 150 / 250 units, with and without NFC
4. personalization/initials upcharge and added lead time
5. production lead time after sample approval
6. packaging options quoted separately: rigid box, dust bag, insert cards
7. whether you can Pantone-match the clay leather to a swatch
8. whether you can embed NTAG215 invisibly and test read reliability through leather plus flap

Optional comparison - custom arrow shape:

Separately, I would also like a quote for a custom arrow-shaped version of the same tag. The arrow shape ties to our brand, but I want to compare it against the standard tag before committing. Please tell me:

1. custom cut-die/tooling cost
2. sample cost and sample lead time for the custom shape
3. MOQ for the custom-shaped tag vs. the standard shape
4. whether the painted edge will hold cleanly around the arrow point, or whether the point is prone to cracking/peeling
5. whether a privacy flap plus clear ID window can work within an arrow shape, or whether the taper makes that impractical
6. where the NTAG215 chip would sit in this shape, and whether it still reads reliably through the leather/flap
7. whether you need dieline artwork from us in AI, PDF, or SVG format, including required dimensions, bleed, and safe-margin specs

If the arrow shape is impractical or low-durability, please say so honestly. I would rather know now.

Required arrow-shape questions:

A. Minimum tip radius: what is the smallest arrow-point radius you can cut in full-grain leather that will NOT crack or peel at the painted edge over normal bag wear? State the radius you recommend.

B. Edge construction at the point: do you recommend painted edge or folded edge for the tapered point, and which holds up better long-term?

C. Body construction: is the flap + ID window a single-layer or multi-layer build, and how does that affect total thickness at the point and at the chip?

D. NFC clearance: confirm the NTAG215 sits at least 15mm from all metal hardware AND is not under the privacy flap. Where exactly will you place it?

E. Tooling + MOQ ceiling: state one-time cut-die cost AND the minimum order quantity for the custom shape. Decision rule on our side: we only advance the arrow if tooling is reasonable and MOQ fits a small validation run.

F. Dieline format + specs: confirm the format you need (AI/PDF/SVG) and give us your required bleed (mm), safe margin (mm), and minimum corner/point radius so we can supply a correct file.

G. Comparison: quote the SAME spec as a standard rounded-rectangle so we can compare cost, MOQ, and lead time directly against the arrow.

Arrow decision rule:

Advance the arrow to a sample only if cut-die tooling and custom-shape MOQ fit a small validation run. Target ceiling: tooling under $300 and MOQ of 150 units or fewer. If the arrow exceeds that, defer it to a future Series 002 limited edition and keep the standard rounded-rectangle as the primary Series 001 sample.

Therrance Carrothers

## Sample Approval Checklist

Judge every sample against one question:

> Does this feel like $75 in the hand?

Approve only if:

- Leather feels and smells like real full-grain leather.
- Supplier confirms grade and origin.
- Clay orange feels rich, warm, and premium.
- Deep black reads giftable/corporate-safe.
- Deboss depth is clean; shallow deboss reads cheap.
- Gold foil, if sampled, feels like an accent, not promo.
- Hardware has real weight and clean finish.
- Painted edge has no cracking, bleeding, or roughness.
- Privacy flap feels premium and snaps/holds cleanly.
- Clear ID window/card slot does not cheapen the object.
- NTAG215 reads reliably through leather and flap on Therrance's iPhone.
- Static Thurr URL opens quickly.
- No visible chip/sticker ruins the premium feel.
- Packaging path makes the $75 target believable.

Reject or iterate if:

- Leather feels like PU, bonded, or promo material.
- Supplier avoids leather grade/origin.
- Orange is too bright or toy-like.
- NFC fails or only works when exposed.
- Hardware is flimsy.
- Edge paint is sloppy.
- The privacy window feels cheap.
- Supplier pushes bulk production before sample approval.
- Arrow shape, if sampled, makes the tag feel gimmicky, fragile, sharp at the point, hard to edge-paint, or unreliable for NFC.
