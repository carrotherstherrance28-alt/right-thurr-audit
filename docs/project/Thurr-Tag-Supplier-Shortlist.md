# Thurr Tag Supplier Shortlist

Date: June 7, 2026

This is a starting shortlist, not an endorsement. Contact candidates, verify capability, request samples, and compare the physical results before any production order.

## Recommendation

Use a three-supplier RFQ first:

1. Jin Sheu.
2. Two vetted Alibaba suppliers with 4.8+ rating and 5+ years in operation.
3. Domestic leather makers only if you want a premium reference sample or the overseas/NFC lane feels weak.

Do not use print-on-demand for the core tag. It is unlikely to support real leather, embedded NFC, edge finishing, custom deboss, numbering, and premium packaging at the quality level needed.

Arrow-shape note: request a custom arrow-shaped version as an optional comparison quote, not as the default Series 001 order. Advance it only if tooling cost, custom-shape MOQ, painted-edge durability, NFC placement, privacy flap feasibility, and sample quality all hold up.

## Leather Tag Candidates

### Jin Sheu

URL: https://www.jinsheu.com/en/category/leather-luggage-tag.html

Relevant product page:

https://www.jinsheu.com/en/product/leather-name-tags-for-luggage.html

Why it is worth contacting:

- Taiwan-based custom promotional products manufacturer with leather luggage tag pages.
- Public product pages show leather travel tags with matching leather strap, metal buckle, clear window, and smooth branding surface.
- Lists customization methods including embossing, debossing, foil stamping, and printing.
- Other Jin Sheu luggage tag pages mention privacy/security flap options and custom size/shape availability.

Best use:

- First RFQ target.
- Good fit for the exact spec: privacy flap, buckle strap, deboss/foil comparison, and production MOQ quote.
- Best first candidate for an optional arrow-shape quote because public pages mention custom size/shape availability.
- Must confirm real full-grain leather and NFC embedding because public pages include genuine leather/PU options and do not lock NFC by default.

Questions to ask:

- Can you make the sample in full-grain real leather, not PU or lower-grade genuine leather?
- Can you embed NTAG215 invisibly and test read reliability through leather plus flap?
- Can you sample rich clay orange and deep black?
- Can you sample gunmetal and gold/brass hardware?
- Can you do wordmark-only deboss lower third plus Series 001 at base?
- Can you quote a custom arrow-shaped version as a comparison, including tooling cost, custom MOQ, painted-edge durability around the point, privacy flap feasibility, NFC chip placement, and dieline requirements?
- What are prices at 100 / 150 / 250 units with and without NFC?

Source notes:

- Jin Sheu describes custom leather luggage/name tags, leather straps with metal buckle, clear windows, emboss/deboss/foil/printing options, privacy flap options on related product pages, and custom leather luggage tag production from Taiwan.

### Vetted Alibaba Suppliers

URL: https://www.alibaba.com/supplier/luggage-tag-manufacturers.html

Why it is worth contacting:

- Best fast lane for cost/MOQ reality.
- Use only suppliers with 4.8+ rating, 5+ years in operation, strong response rate, and relevant leather luggage tag examples.
- Alibaba's luggage-tag supplier category includes manufacturers across leather, PU, silicone, and PVC, with customization filters and supplier indicators.

Best use:

- Select two suppliers to send the exact RFQ.
- Use replies to benchmark cost at 100 / 150 / 250 units with and without NFC.
- Use the optional arrow quote to learn whether overseas suppliers can make the directional shape without turning the product into a promotional novelty.

Questions to ask:

- Can you produce full-grain real leather, not PU/bonded/generic genuine leather?
- Can you embed NTAG215 invisibly inside the tag?
- Can you make a snap-closed privacy flap over clear ID window/card slot?
- Can you do painted edge, lower-third deboss, Series 001 base mark, and Pantone clay orange?
- Can you quote a custom arrow-shaped version and explain cut-die/tooling cost, MOQ, and NFC/privacy-flap feasibility?
- Can you quote packaging separately?

Risk:

- Many low-cost luggage tag listings are promotional-grade PU or leatherette.
- Do not let low unit pricing pull the product downmarket.
- Treat any arrow quote that skips tooling, edge-paint, or NFC placement detail as weak.

## Decision Filter

Advance a supplier only if they can answer these clearly:

- sample cost
- sample timeline
- real leather options
- NFC embedding approach
- deboss/numbering approach
- hardware options
- packaging options
- MOQ after sample approval
- unit costs at 100, 150, 250
- defect/replacement policy
- optional arrow-shape quote: tooling cost, custom MOQ, edge durability, privacy flap feasibility, NFC placement, and dieline requirements

Do not advance a supplier if:

- they only offer PU/leatherette
- they can only attach a visible NFC sticker
- they cannot provide sample photos
- they avoid MOQ/unit cost questions
- they push bulk production before sample approval
- they say custom shape is easy but cannot explain die/tooling, edge finish, NFC placement, or flap constraints
