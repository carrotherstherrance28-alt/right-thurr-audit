# Thurr Tag Corporate Gifting One-Pager

Date: June 7, 2026

Use this as the first corporate gifting pitch. It is written for warm outreach, LinkedIn follow-up, email follow-up, or a simple PDF/Canva one-pager.

## Positioning

Headline:

> Premium gifts your clients actually carry.

Subheadline:

> Thurr Tag is a premium leather NFC bag tag that introduces your people, your brand, or your offer every time someone taps it.

Short pitch:

> Most corporate gifts get forgotten. Thurr Tag lives on the bag, opens a digital profile or custom welcome page with one tap, and keeps pointing people back to the right connection long after the gift is received.

## What It Is

Thurr Tag is a premium leather NFC bag/travel tag with:

- real leather construction
- embedded NFC chip
- co-branded or quiet debossed face
- custom tap destination
- numbered Series 001 option
- gift-ready packaging
- QR fallback card
- optional recipient message or team profile

It is not GPS, tracking, AirTag, or a disposable promotional item.

## Best Use Cases

- Client appreciation gifts
- Founder and executive gifts
- Sales team travel kits
- Conference and event gifts
- New hire onboarding
- Referral partner gifts
- VIP customer experiences

## Tap Destination Options

Each tag can point to:

- recipient profile
- team directory
- sales rep vCard
- booking link
- referral page
- client thank-you message
- event landing page
- Thurr-built lead capture page

Series 001 keeps the technology simple: the chip points to a URL. The destination can change later without changing the physical tag.

## Discovery Questions

Ask corporate prospects:

1. Who would receive the tags: clients, team, event guests, or referral partners?
2. How many recipients are realistic for a pilot: 25, 50, or 100+?
3. What should the tap open: profile, booking link, thank-you page, offer, referral flow, or event page?
4. Should the tag be co-branded or more subtle?
5. Is this for a specific event/date or general gifting?
6. Who signs off on the gifting budget?
7. Would a paid sample or prototype help the decision?

## Internal Close Criteria

A corporate lead counts toward the production gate only if one of these happens:

- signed purchase order
- paid deposit
- written approval from a buyer with budget
- committed pilot tied to a real event or gifting date

A warm DM, casual interest, or "sounds cool" does not trigger production.
