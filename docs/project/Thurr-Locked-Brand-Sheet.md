# Thurr Locked Brand Sheet

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This sheet is the current lock for Thurr, Right Thurr, Thurr Solutions, and Thurr Tag. If older planning docs conflict with this sheet, this sheet wins.

## Brand Architecture

- Thurr: the brand/world and umbrella.
- Therrance "Thurr" Carrothers: the person, founder, operator, and face.
- Right Thurr: the movement, voice, content lane, and attitude layer. It is not a separate company or a second main logo system.
- Thurr Solutions: the B2B company for branding, AI, lead generation, automation, setup consultations, and business growth.
- Thurr Tag: the first physical product and artifact.

## Naming Lock

- Product name: Thurr Tag
- Drop name: Thurr Tag - Series 001
- Do not use: Thurr Tag - Series 001 Access Tag
- Category copy: premium leather NFC tag for people in motion
- Product line: Tap. Connect. Thurr you go.

Access is a concept in the copy. It is not part of the product name.

## Positioning

- Mission: I point people in the right direction.
- Product promise: Point every tap in the right direction.
- Signature sign-off: Thurr you go.

Do not use "Thurr you go" as the main positioning line by itself. It works best after the offer is clear.

## Approved Phrases

- Right Thurr
- Tap Right Thurr
- Thurr you go
- in motion
- Series 001
- HURR OR THURR, kept together as a phrase-world, sticker, packaging, and interior-detail treatment
- THURR or thurr, as the simplified brand/product word treatment
- HURR, only inside the intentional HURR OR THURR phrase

## Removed Phrases

- accidental hurr
- Be Thurr
- Already Thurr
- Who Thurr?

Do not revive these unless the phrase system is intentionally reopened.

Physical tag copy lock:

- Core Founders Edition face: quiet thurr/THURR word treatment or small mark
- Supplier sample face: wordmark-only thurr/THURR, lower third
- HURR OR THURR works on stickers, packaging interiors, and inside-flap details, not the default leather face
- Secondary details: Tap Right Thurr, Series 001, RT monogram/T mark on strap
- Optional arrow-shaped/directional prototype may be quoted because it fits the mission, but it is not public launch copy or the default Series 001 shape until a physical sample proves premium feel, durability, NFC reliability, and production cost.
- Product name remains Thurr Tag.

IG, TikTok, and X point to the hub. LinkedIn may point to the operator/business path when needed.

## Thurr Tag Guardrails

- Waitlist only before sample.
- No paid orders before holding a sample that feels worth $75.
- No ship-date promise before production is scheduled.
- No GPS, tracking, AirTag-like, or smart-tag claims.
- Do not lead with lost bag recovery.
- Do not call it merch in public copy.
- Stickers are a companion SKU, not the hero.

Validation gate:

- 200 qualified waitlist signups, or
- 5 paid preorders after sample approval, or
- 1 corporate gifting commitment.

## Pricing

- Founders Edition: $75
- Personalized Edition: $99
- Sticker Pack: $20
- Corporate Pilot: 25 tags / $1,875
- Corporate Campaign: 50 tags / $3,500
- Partner: 100+ custom

## Logo Hierarchy

- Primary mark target: lowercase thurr wordmark when that asset exists.
- Secondary mark: RT monogram.
- Right Thurr wordmark: phrase/content mark and existing visual asset, not a separate brand competing with Thurr.
- Physical Thurr Tag mockups should show wordmark-only tag faces for the supplier sample. HURR OR THURR should remain visible in the sticker/phrase system.

Do not create more logo systems while the product is still validating.
