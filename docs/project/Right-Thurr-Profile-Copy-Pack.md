# Right Thurr Profile Copy Pack

Last updated: 2026-05-26

## Voice Standard

Thurr's public profile voice should sound direct, warm, practical, and system-minded.

Use this pattern:

1. What I build.
2. Who it helps.
3. Why it matters.
4. What the next move is.

Avoid:

- Generic AI guru language.
- Guaranteed income claims.
- Fake scarcity.
- Over-polished corporate language.
- Making Right Thurr, Thurr Solutions, and Thurr sound like three unrelated brands.
