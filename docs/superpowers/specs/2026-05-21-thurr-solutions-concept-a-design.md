# Thurr Solutions Homepage v2 Concept A Design

Date: 2026-05-21

## Approved Direction

Concept A is approved with the `Brand Conversion Studio` variant.

The homepage leads with `Thurr Solutions` only. Right Thurr is not shown in the homepage header. The page should feel like a clean B2B brand-growth and lead-conversion agency for local service businesses, with enough operator precision to prove the system is real.

The site should not feel like an AI tool showcase, a travel brand, or a loud personal/product brand. AI is an enabler inside the system, not the main product being sold.

## Positioning

Primary idea:

> Build the brand system behind booked work.

Thurr Solutions builds brand-focused websites, lead pathways, AI commercial assets, and response systems for local service businesses that need more than attention. They need captured leads, fast follow-up, and a system that keeps opportunities from slipping.

The homepage story should move in this order:

1. Brand credibility: make the business look like the obvious choice.
2. Lead conversion: route attention into forms, calls, bookings, and qualified next steps.
3. Response systems: text-back, CRM tracking, follow-up, and chatbot support only where useful.
4. Operations and safety: available as proof and sub-page depth, not the front-page pitch.

## Visual Direction

Use the existing OPERATOR CORE tokens:

- Warm paper background.
- Ink text and hard borders.
- Rust as the primary conversion color.
- Slab headlines, Inter body, mono labels.
- Hard offset shadows.
- 4px radii.

The page should feel premium and controlled, not decorative. Use operational diagrams, proof strips, and structured system language instead of abstract AI graphics, generic icons, or fake SaaS dashboards.

## Voice Rules

Use short, direct copy.

Prefer:

- `booked work`
- `lead pathways`
- `response systems`
- `follow-up`
- `missed-call text-back`
- `CRM tracking`
- `brand system`
- `local service businesses`

Avoid:

- `unlock your potential`
- `transform your business`
- generic `AI-powered solutions`
- tool-name dumping
- travel references
- Right Thurr-forward language on the homepage

## Homepage Section Strategy

The original nine-section structure still applies, but the emphasis changes:

1. Hero: Brand Conversion Studio direction.
2. Problem: paid attention leaks through weak web presence, slow response, and untracked follow-up.
3. System: Brand, Lead Pathway, Response System.
4. What We Build: Brand & Web, Lead Pathways, Response Systems, Operations.
5. Process: Audit, Build, Launch, Operate.
6. Work: Restore Contracting first, HeartPathBloom only when framed through `/compliance-grade`.
7. Packages: Audit visible at $250; other pricing hidden.
8. About: solo operator credibility, not big-agency theater.
9. Final CTA: Lead Flow Audit.

## Government-Ready Recommendation

Add government contracting as a secondary premium lane, not as the main homepage promise.

Recommended label:

> Government-Ready Brand & Lead Systems

This should live under Operations, Compliance-Grade, or a future `/government-ready` page. It should not replace the homepage hero, because the main offer is still brand growth and lead conversion for local service businesses.

Offer this lane as:

- Government-ready website refreshes.
- Capability statement design and copy support.
- NAICS and service-positioning research.
- SAM.gov registration checklist guidance.
- Contract opportunity monitoring setup.
- CRM pipeline for bids, RFPs, agency contacts, and follow-up.
- Compliance-aware intake and document organization.

Avoid making award guarantees or implying legal/compliance certification. Do not use language like `we win you government contracts`. Keep the pitch practical: Thurr Solutions helps service businesses look credible, organize their contract-readiness materials, and build the follow-up system around public-sector opportunities.
