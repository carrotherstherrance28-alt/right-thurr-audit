# Right Thurr Phrase Bank

## Status

Working draft for internal content planning.

Do not publish, print, or put these on merch until Thurr approves the exact public wording for the specific use.

## Core Positioning

- Free Game for ambitious operators.
- Get put on to what moves you forward.
- Tools, habits, setups, and people worth knowing.
- The next move is right thurr.
- Built for people trying to move better.

## Repeatable Content Lines

- What are you trying to get put on to right now?
- Here is the setup.
- Here is the move.
- Save this for when you need it.
- This is not a hack. It is a system.
- Keep the tool. Build the habit.
- Better inputs. Better movement.
- The tool only matters if you know where it fits.
- Travel lighter. Move sharper.
- Your setup is part of your discipline.

## Thurr-Isms To Test Carefully

- You got to be thurr.
- Subscribe button right thurr.
- I am Thurr.
- Be Thurr.
- Thurr you go.
- Thurr Way.
- Right thurr if you need it.
- Put-ons right thurr.

Use these lightly at first. They work best as captions, stickers, short CTAs, or community naming, not as the full brand promise.

## AI / Tools Language

- Tool put-on.
- Setup breakdown.
- AI that fits the workflow.
- Not every tool needs to become your stack.
- Before I recommend it, I check if it still holds up.
- What it does, who it helps, and how I would use it.
- Tools do not replace taste, consistency, or follow-through.

## Fitness / Healthy Eats Language

- Move first.
- Keep the routine travel-proof.
- No-perfect-day workout.
- Eat like you still have to perform.
- Simple meals. Better decisions.
- Healthy enough to keep moving.

Keep this general and personal. Do not frame posts as medical, nutrition, injury, or transformation advice.

## Travel / Network Language

- Move with intention.
- Be useful in every room.
- The city teaches if you pay attention.
- Pack what keeps you consistent.
- Good network, good movement.
- Know where you are going and who you can help.

## Community Language

- Free Game Circle.
- Weekly Put-On.
- Setup Sunday.
- Tool Board.
- Useful People Board.
- Wins and Fixes.
- What should we put people on to next?

Avoid promising daily support, private coaching, guaranteed access, or paid value before the community has proof.

## Do Not Use

- Guaranteed income claims.
- Guaranteed fitness/health claims.
- Fake scarcity.
- Fake testimonials.
- Synthetic user endorsements.
- Client-sensitive examples without approval.
- "AI will do it all for you."
- "Passive income" as a promise.
- "Get rich" framing.
- Anything that makes Right Thurr sound like Thurr Solutions' B2B homepage.
